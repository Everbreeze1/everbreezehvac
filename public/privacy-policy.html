Privacy Policy

Everbreeze Heating And Air® respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website, booking forms, phone calls, emails, and text messages.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, appointment preferences, and details about your heating or air conditioning service request when you contact us or submit a form.

How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, schedule and confirm appointments, provide HVAC service, send service reminders, communicate about your request, and improve customer service.

Text Messages and Consent

If you provide your phone number and voluntarily opt in, you agree to receive text messages from Everbreeze Heating And Air® about your inquiry, appointment scheduling, reminders, service updates, and customer care. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to cancel. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Mobile opt-in information and text message consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers only as needed to operate our business, schedule service, communicate with you, or comply with legal requirements.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards to protect submitted information. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
